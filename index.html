        The board is nine rows tall. On a phone lying down there is no way to fit that without shrinking the gems past a comfortable tap.

        ×2 chain

        • Swap two neighbouring gems to line up three or more of a colour.
        • Every colour has its own shape, so the board reads without relying on hue.
        • Four in a row leaves a striped gem that clears its whole line.
        • An L or T leaves a bomb that clears the surrounding 3×3.
        • Five in a line leaves a prism — swap it onto any colour to wipe that colour off the board.
        • Cascades multiply your score, up to ×8.
        • Finish a level to earn an item, and land a ×5 chain for a bomb. Items cost no move — and they are earned fresh every run, so the board is the same climb for everyone.
