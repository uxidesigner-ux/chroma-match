# Attempt 6 — open wrap + bang + hanging locks

Checkpoint of the remake against the stacked-pair original (long wave, black
knit). 5c is not the proportion source.

## What changed from 5c

5c was overlapping ellipsoids remeshed into two temple bulbs with a trapped
face. This pass:

- opens the face (the head is a round clay volume again)
- puts a right-side part and a diagonal bang across the brow
- hangs length beside the shoulders instead of inflating the skull
- shortens the neck and sits a crew knit under the chin

## What the large compare still shows

Hair is not the long-wave wrap. It reads as a skull cap, a forehead visor, and
two hanging masses. The part rim is jagged. Temple nicks remain. Lengths do
not carry the original’s S-curve body over the shoulders. The back is designed
(not in the crop): smooth occiput, two side tails, no central rat-tail.

Face / neck / knit moved more than the hair. Visual approval is not claimed.
